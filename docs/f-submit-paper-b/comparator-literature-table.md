## Candidate-accounting note (frozen 2026-11-19)

- **Candidates found:** 39 total (10 selected + 29 rejected). Source: the local literature tree plus search pass of 2026-11-19.
- **Verified:** deep verification covered the 10 selected plus 4 reserves (Faster and Stronger; Reconsidering the energy efficiency of SNNs; STA; Spiking PointNet) = 14 papers. The remaining rejects were screened from supplied metadata with spot checks; several carry preprint/unconfirmed-venue caveats already flagged in the source records.
- **Verification method:** each of the 10 selected papers verified via WebSearch and direct retrieval (WebFetch was network-blocked for arxiv.org/neurips.cc, so export.arxiv.org was fetched via curl for arXiv metadata). Identity evidence: publisher pages (MDPI), arXiv citation metadata, iclr.cc/icml.cc virtual poster pages, OpenReview/GitHub official repos, DOI records (ScienceDirect, ACM DL). Index evidence: conference papers from the CCF 2022 recommended list (ICML/AAAI/IJCAI = class A, re-verified this pass) plus official proceedings listings; journals from publisher/aggregator pages (JCR and Scopus not directly accessible — all quartile/IF figures are labelled as coming from publisher or third-party pages and should be re-checked in JCR before citing).
- **Selection logic:** exactly 10 chosen to cover every claim axis of an ANN-to-SNN conversion-analysis-for-graphs manuscript: graph-niche prior art (Spiking PointCNN = closest prior art, SpikingGCN = opposite-route baseline, SpikeGCL = spike-graph expressiveness, SGNNBench = spiking-GNN evaluation, MK-SGN = GCN-to-SGN conversion benchmark) + conversion theory (QCFS = zero expected error, Offset Spikes = existing error identity, SpikeZIP-TF = lossless equivalence, Adaptive Calibration = unevenness error + adaptive timesteps novelty risk, DCGS = differential coding/timestep allocation). Two 2022 papers (QCFS, SpikingGCN) retained as mandatory baselines despite the 2023-2026 preference.
- **Corrections applied to source records:** (1) SpikingGCN authors are Zulun Zhu, Jiaying Peng, Jintang Li, Liang Chen, Qi Yu, Siqiang Luo — the tentative 'Yuanpeiqi Guo?' is wrong; (2) ICLR is NOT in the CCF 2022 recommended list, so 'CCF-A' labels for ICLR papers (QCFS, Offset Spikes, SpikeGCL, STA) must not be cited without checking a newer CCF edition — ICLR remains field-leading by reputation; (3) SGNNBench: arXiv comments field has NO acceptance note and the abstract says 18 datasets (record claimed 20); the claimed NeurIPS poster is unconfirmed — treat as arXiv preprint; (4) MK-SGN is online 2025 (Neurocomputing vol. 662 listed 2026) and is knowledge-distillation based (GCN teacher to SGN student), not weight-transfer conversion; (5) the Nature 629:1031-1036 record is 'Low-latency automotive vision with event cameras' (Gehrig & Scaramuzza) — the local literature tree's 'DAGr' identity is wrong, as the record itself flags.
- **Reserve order (promote first if slots free or the table expands):** (1) Faster and Stronger (AAAI 2025) — closest theory alternate, rejected solely on the exactly-10 cap; (2) Reconsidering the energy efficiency of SNNs (arXiv:2409.08290) — must-cite if the manuscript makes energy claims, but unrefereed; (3) STA (ICLR 2024) for attention-like aggregation conversion; (4) Spiking PointNet (NeurIPS 2023) for error-vs-timestep behavior.
