# Comparator Literature Table — Paper A (Reproducibility/evaluation audit of trained spiking GNNs)

- **Frozen selection date:** 2026-11-19
- **Scope:** Comparator papers for the reproducibility/evaluation audit of trained spiking GNNs, across three axes: (1) reproducibility-audit methodology; (2) journal reusability-report genre + protocol transportability; (3) graph-domain reproduction studies, stochastic-variation statistics, split transportability, GNN benchmark commensurability, and spiking-GNN/neuromorphic evaluation.
- **Candidate accounting:** 29 candidates, all identity-verified via WebSearch/WebFetch against publisher pages (ScienceDirect, Nature/Springer, proceedings.mlr.press, papers.nips.cc, ACM DL, openreview/arXiv), DBLP, and index/metric sources. All 29 exist as claimed by title. Exactly 10 comparators selected per topical fit; 19 rejected as redundant, tangential, or out-of-genre (see Candidate Accounting below). No paper outside the verification result appears in this file.

### Rejected candidates (n = 19) and dispositions

1. **Unpaired deep-learning approaches for holographic image reconstruction** (Zhang, Ritschel, Villanueva-Perez; Nature Machine Intelligence 6(3):284-290, 2024, s42256-024-00798-7) — VERIFIED real (NMI SCIE Q1), but a third NMI reusability report is redundant with the two selected genre comparators (So = flagship format, Heirman = protocol re-run + external data); imaging domain less central.
2. **The Data Provenance Initiative: A Large Scale Audit of Dataset Licensing & Attribution in AI** (Longpre et al., arXiv 2310.16787, 2023) — VERIFIED, but provenance/licensing focus is tangential to reproducibility auditing of trained model artifacts; primary record arXiv-only.
3. **RECLAIM: Can Agents Reproduce the Claims of Machine Learning Papers?** (arXiv 2609.28850, 2026) — VERIFIED (Salunkhe, Ding, Verma, Kindratenko; 100 NeurIPS 2025 papers; 41% run / 27% retrain / 15% reimplement tiers). Close methodological fit but arXiv-only preprint; automated-agent claim reproduction is adjacent to, not the same genre. **Backup.**
4. **Fairness in Link Prediction Beyond Demographic Parity: A Reproducibility Study** (arXiv 2608.09899) — VERIFIED; actually TMLR-published 05/2026 (candidate metadata said arXiv-only). Graph-domain reproducibility study but fairness-claim class; graph-reproduction slot filled by Nijdam et al. (TMLR).
5. **Spiking Graph Neural Network on Riemannian Manifolds** (Sun, Huang, Wan, Peng, Yu; NeurIPS 2024, arXiv 2410.17941) — VERIFIED. Audited-method candidate (code-releasing trained SGNN), not a comparator for the audit methodology; its evaluation axis is covered by SGNNBench.
6. **Continuous Spiking Graph Neural Networks / COS-GNN** (Yin et al., arXiv 2404.01897, 2024) — VERIFIED as arXiv preprint (no peer-reviewed venue confirmed). Audited-method candidate, not a comparator; no SCI/index standing.

8. **Scaling Up Dynamic Graph Representation Learning via Spiking Neural Networks / SpikeNet** (Jintang Li et al.; AAAI 2023, AAAI 37(7):8588-8596, DOI 10.1609/aaai.v37i7.26034) — VERIFIED (code EdisonLeeeee/SpikeNet). No index status or impact factor is asserted beyond what the cited sources state.
